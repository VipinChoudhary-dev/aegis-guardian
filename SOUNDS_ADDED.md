## Sound System

### Available Sounds:

1. **playClick()** - Short, pleasant click sound
2. **playSuccess()** - Musical success chime (C-E-G notes)
3. **playNotification()** - Alert notification sound
4. **playWarning()** - Warning beep (3 times)
5. **playEmergencyAlarm()** - Continuous siren (5 seconds)

## Technical Details

### Web Audio API:
- Uses native browser audio
- No external files needed
- Lightweight and fast
- Works on all devices

### Sound Characteristics:
- **Click:** 800Hz sine wave, 0.1s duration
- **Success:** 3 notes (C-E-G), 0.3s each
- **Alarm:** Alternating 900Hz/700Hz, 5 seconds
- **Volume:** 30% (not too loud)
